# Responsible business model (hypotheses, not forecasts)

Possible revenue: transparent group subscription or per-group admin fee, optional payment-processing fee (if partnered with licensed payment providers), compliant loan interest only for a legally authorized lending model, and disclosed referral/administration revenue from licensed insurance partners if permitted. Avoid selling personal transaction data or hidden charges.

Illustrative scenario (not actual users): 100 stokvel groups x R99/group/month = R9,900 gross recurring monthly platform fees before tax, payment processing, infrastructure, customer support and defaults. Lending interest is not pure profit: subtract funding cost, delinquencies, regulation, operations and credit losses.

Inflation management: show contributions in today's purchasing-power terms, optional group-approved annual contribution review, liquidity reserve, diversified regulated savings products if appropriate. Never imply speculative crypto yield or guaranteed inflation beating returns.
