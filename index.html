The problem

Stokvels run on cash and blind trust.

One or two people hold the money. Members can't check what happened to it.

Money goes “missing”

Members who paid get told they didn't.

Loans on a handshake

Terms are agreed verbally, then quietly changed.

Silent borrowing

Admins can dip into the pot without telling the group.

Messy shared costs

Function, groceries, funeral support: everyone chases everyone.

Core features

Four ways to save with confidence.

Transparency

Every contribution, loan and withdrawal is recorded in a shared ledger members can review.

Security

Shared funds require multiple signers to approve withdrawals, so no one person controls the pot.

Accessibility

A clear, mobile-first dashboard helps every member follow balances, requests and group activity.

Interest gain

Track agreed loan interest and repayments as they contribute to group savings. Earnings depend on loan terms and are not guaranteed.

How it works

From first contribution to live audit trail.

  1. Create a stokvel and invite members.
  2. Contribute monthly in USDT, or cash confirmed by the member.
  3. Split a bill like the year-end function. Everyone pays their share.
  4. Request a loan. Two members approve and funds move on-chain.
  5. Cheating is blocked. Unapproved withdrawals alert everyone.
