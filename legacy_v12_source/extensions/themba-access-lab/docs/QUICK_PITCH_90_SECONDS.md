# 90-second W3Node pitch — Themba

**0–15 seconds:** "In South Africa, stokvels help people save together, but members still need easy ways to see contributions, payout order and group decisions. Transparency shouldn't depend on trusting one spreadsheet or one person's phone."

**15–35 seconds:** "Themba is the governance layer for a savings circle. Members can use a phone browser or a familiar USSD menu, in their preferred language, to understand their group's records. A treasurer can record contributions and members see statuses clearly."

**35–60 seconds:** "For rotating payouts, the requester cannot approve their own payout. Two distinct eligible group members must authorize it. We display the future contributions at risk and a separate limited reserve, so members don't approve blindly. The two approvers are not automatically personal guarantors."

**60–75 seconds:** "If a group chooses blockchain custody, the same restriction needs to live in a verified smart contract and every execution needs a public transaction proof. Everyday users shouldn't have to buy crypto or learn gas fees; an authorized partner can provide a familiar experience, while risks, approvals and custody stay transparent."

**75–90 seconds:** "We begin by working alongside existing stokvel bank accounts and charge groups for optional advanced administration, reconciliation and reporting. Today we can demonstrate the accessibility and local governance workflows. We still have to finish live provider and blockchain integration before real funds are involved."

**Do not claim a working contract-level approval restriction until verified testnet evidence exists.**
