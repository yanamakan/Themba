BLOCKCHAIN, WITHOUT CRYPTO CLASSES

Make the technology invisible—not the risks

What the member experiences

  • Choose a language; sign into an accessible account.
  • Check a contribution or scheduled payout using the web, SMS or USSD.
  • See an approval request and understand the amount and consequences.
  • Receive a truthful status: reported, verified, authorized or settled.

The user should not be required to buy tokens or manage gas. Phone-only financial approvals need strong identity assurance before going live.

What happens underneath

  • Bank mode: a licensed payment partner verifies ordinary rand settlement; it does not become magically controlled by a smart contract.
  • Optional testnet mode: Sepolia transfers and approvals are visible on a blockchain explorer.
  • Future production: smart-wallet abstractions and gas sponsorship require secure custody, consent, recovery and compliance.
  • Security: a contract/guard must enforce requester exclusion and approved destinations, not only the web screen.

Sepolia evidence viewer · Link only

Paste a transaction hash provided by the teammate operating the testnet deployment. This form only constructs an explorer link and does not verify the transaction or its authorization rules. Never paste private keys or seed phrases.

Critical limitation: our live browser preview and USSD callback do not execute a blockchain transaction. Yana's Supabase application and Astra's proposed payout contract are separate. Before calling this an on-chain protection system, demonstrate a deployed testnet policy, its verified address, a rejected self-approval and a successful two-approver execution, with corresponding public transaction proof.
